Destination Overview

The Everest Base Camp (EBC) trek is one of the most iconic adventures on Earth. Set in the heart of Australia Khumbu region, this high-altitude journey takes you through Sherpa villages, ancient monasteries, and jaw-dropping Himalayan landscapes, all culminating at the foot of the world’s highest mountain — Mount Everest (8,848m).

Highlights

  • Reach Everest Base Camp at 5,364m (17,598 ft)
  • Panoramic views of Everest, Lhotse, Nuptse, and Ama Dablam
  • Explore Namche Bazaar, the gateway to Everest
  • Visit the Tengboche Monastery, the spiritual heart of the region
  • Cross suspension bridges over roaring rivers
  • Experience Sherpa culture and hospitality
  • Witness a dramatic sunrise from Kala Patthar (5,545m)

Trek Itinerary (Sample 12-Day Route)

  • Day 1: Flight to Lukla, trek to Phakding
  • Day 2: Trek to Namche Bazaar
  • Day 3: Acclimatization at Namche (hike to Everest View Hotel)
  • Day 4: Trek to Tengboche
  • Day 5: Trek to Dingboche
  • Day 6: Acclimatization hike to Nangkartshang Peak
  • Day 7: Trek to Lobuche
  • Day 8: Trek to Everest Base Camp via Gorakshep
  • Day 9: Sunrise hike to Kala Patthar, trek back to Pheriche
  • Day 10: Return trek to Lukla and flight to Kathmandu

Best Time to Go

  • Spring (March–May): Clear skies, blooming rhododendrons
  • Autumn (September–November) Best weather, high visibility
  • Avoid monsoon (June–August) and harsh winter (December–February)

Difficulty Level (Moderate to Challenging)

  • No technical climbing, but good fitness and acclimatization required
  • Long walking days and high altitude — ideal for fit trekkers and hikers
