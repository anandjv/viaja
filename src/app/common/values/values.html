Our Values

The principles that guide every Viaja adventure

01

Safety First

All guides are wilderness first responder certified with 10+ years experience. We maintain 24/7 emergency support.

02

Sustainable Travel

Carbon-neutral trips since 2018. We've removed 100% of single-use plastics and direct 5% of profits to conservation.

03

Community Impact

85% of our staff are local hires. We've built 3 schools and support 15+ family run teahouses in Australia.

04

Authentic Experiences

Our trips focus on meaningful cultural connections, Average group size of 8 ensures personal attention.
